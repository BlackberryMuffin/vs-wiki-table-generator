<!--T:1-->
'''Clothes''' are decorative items that change the player's appearance when equipped. A clothing item can protect a player from the cold depending on its condition.

<!--T:2-->
__TOC__

== Obtaining == <!--T:46-->

<!--T:47-->
Some clothes can be crafted using {{ll|Cloth|cloth}}, {{ll|Leather|leather}}, or other materials. Most clothes are not craftable, and can only be found as loot in {{ll|Ruin|ruins}}, or bought from {{ll|Trader|traders}}. Players are given a set of starter clothes for free when they choose their {{ll|Players#Classes|class}}.

<!--T:48-->
Many clothing items can only be crafted by the [[Classes#Tailor|Tailor]] player class. Tailors also receive a 10% bonus to armor durability, but this doesn't affect clothing durability.<ref>Tailors get -0.1 <code>"armorDurabilityLoss"</code>, which is used only in <code>DamageItem()</code> in <code>ItemWearable.cs</code> for armor.</ref>

== Usage == <!--T:49-->

<!--T:50-->
=== Warmth ===
:''Main page: {{ll|Temperature#Body_temperature|Body temperature}}''

<!--T:51-->
[[File:Worn clothing example.png|390px|thumb|alt=Hover tooltip for worn hunter boots|Clothing item tooltip showing condition, warmth value, and max warmth]]

<!--T:52-->
Wearing warm clothes protects the player from the cold. Without clothing, players begin to take damage from the cold when the outside temperature is below 0°C (with default world settings). Each piece of worn clothing offsets the effective temperature by the amount shown in green when hovering the mouse pointer over the item.

<!--T:53-->
For example, if the player wears a full set of {{ll|Fur clothing set|fur clothes}} in "Good" condition, the combined warmth buff will be 3 + 2 + 1.5 = +6.5°C, allowing the player to withstand outside temperatures as cold as -6.5°C without taking damage.

<!--T:54-->
Hovering over a clothing item also shows its condition and max warmth. The warmth value is calculated from the max warmth and condition using this function:<ref><code>GetWarmth()</code> in <code>ItemWearable.cs</code></ref>

<!--T:55-->
<code>warmth = maxWarmth * min(2*condition, 100%)</code>

<!--T:56-->
Clothing with 50% or more condition gives its max warmth value. Below 50% condition, warmth decreases as condition degrades, and is reduced to +0°C at 0% condition.

<!--T:57-->
Some clothing items are purely decorative and don't give any warmth benefit; hovering over them doesn't show any condition or warmth value.

=== Damage and repair === <!--T:58-->

<!--T:59-->
Clothes slowly degrade while being worn,<ref><code>updateWearableConditions()</code> in <code>ItemWearable.cs</code></ref> decreasing their condition by 100% after 54 days, or 6 months (with default world settings). Clothes don't degrade while not being worn, or while the player is standing still.

<!--T:60-->
Damaged clothing can be repaired by 10% using {{ll|Twine|flax twine}}, by 50% using {{ll|Linen|linen}}, or by 75% using a {{ll|Sewing_kit|sewing kit}}. To do so, hold the repair item with the mouse pointer and left-click the clothing item in your inventory or character screen.

<!--T:61-->
Clothes don't take damage from enemy attacks, unlike armor. Clothes that reach 0% condition aren't destroyed, but offer no warmth bonus.

<!--T:62-->
Because 4 twine can be crafted into 1 linen sheet, it's more efficient to use linen sheets or sewing kits for repairing. Clothing condition doesn't affect warmth until it reaches 50%, so repairing isn't necessary before then. To conserve materials, you can take off warm clothes during the summer months, when cold isn't a problem.

=== Other uses === <!--T:63-->

<!--T:64-->
Clothes with "Tattered" in their name can be crafted into {{ll|Bandage|clean bandages}} which heal the player, regardless of their condition.

<!--T:65-->
Some clothing items can be sold to {{ll|Trader|traders}} in exchange for {{ll|Rusty gear|rusty gears}}. The item's condition doesn't matter, and traders will buy clothes in any condition for the same price.

=== Warmest clothing combination === <!--T:69-->

<!--T:70-->
Combining the warmest item in each clothing category gives an outfit with +19.5°C, but requires several items which can only be found as random loot. The warmest outfit which can be crafted by a Tailor gives +18.1°C. A non-Tailor player can craft an outfit which gives +12.75°C.
